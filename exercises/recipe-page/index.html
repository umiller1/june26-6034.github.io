Pumpkin Biscoff Ice Cream Sandwiches

This no-churn ice cream bar layers a buttery Biscoff cookie crust on the bottom and top of a creamy pumpkin spice ice cream center, swirled with warmed Biscoff spread and cookie crumbs. No ice cream maker needed! Just whip, layer, and freeze. It’s the perfect make-ahead dessert for when you want something pumpkin spiced without turning on the oven.

PREP TIME 25 mins   FREEZE TIME 12 hrs

TOTAL TIME 12 hrs 25 mins   YIELDS 8

Ingredients

BOTTOM AND TOP CRUST

  • 2 sleeves Biscoff cookies, 8.8 ounces or 250 grams each, divided
  • 12 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted, divided

PUMPKIN ICE CREAM

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/4 cup Biscoff spread, warmed
  • 1/4 cup Biscoff cookie crumbs

Instructions

MAKE THE BOTTOM CRUST

  1. Line the bottom of a 9×9-inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. In a food processor, add half of the Biscoff cookies (1 pack) and pulse until finely ground into crumbs.
  3. Add the crumbs to a bowl, then add half of the melted butter and stir with a spoon until fully combined and the mixture looks like wet sand. Press it firmly into the bottom of the prepared pan, using the bottom of a measuring cup to pack it into an even layer, then transfer to the freezer while you make the ice cream.
  4. In the food processor, add the remaining Biscoff cookies and pulse until finely ground into crumbs, then transfer to a ziplock bag and set aside at room temperature. You’ll mix this with the remaining butter the next day to make the top crust.

MAKE THE PUMPKIN ICE CREAM

  1. In a large mixing bowl, add the heavy cream and vanilla extract, then whip with a hand mixer on medium speed until stiff peaks form.
  2. Add the condensed milk to the whipped cream, then whisk on low speed until fully incorporated.
  3. Add the pumpkin puree and pumpkin pie spice to the bowl, then whisk again on low speed until smooth and fully combined.

ASSEMBLE AND FREEZE

  1. Remove the pan with the bottom crust from the freezer, then pour half of the pumpkin ice cream mixture over it and spread into an even layer with a rubber spatula.
  2. Drizzle the warmed Biscoff spread over the ice cream layer, then sprinkle the Biscoff cookie crumbs evenly over the top.
  3. Pour the remaining pumpkin ice cream mixture over the Biscoff layer and spread into an even layer with a spatula.
  4. Place a piece of saran wrap directly on top of the ice cream, pressing it down gently to cover the entire surface, then freeze overnight.
  5. The next day, prepare the top crust: add the reserved Biscoff crumbs to a bowl, then add the remaining melted butter and stir with a spoon until fully combined and the mixture looks like wet sand.
  6. Remove the saran wrap, then pour the crumb mixture over the ice cream layer and quickly spread it into an even layer with a spatula, working fast since the mixture firms up quickly.
  7. Lift the dessert out using the parchment paper, then cut down the middle with a sharp knife, cleaning the knife between each cut. Then cut each half into 4 to 5 rectangular pieces, for 8 to 10 pieces total, then enjoy!

Notes

Q: How should I store these in the freezer?

A: Once cut, transfer the pieces to an airtight container, layering parchment paper between them so they don’t stick together. Store in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
